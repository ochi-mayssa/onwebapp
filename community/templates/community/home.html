FAQ

Common Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is the difference between the three packages?
The Enterprise Package ($2,499) includes everything in the Startup Package plus SEO setup and a built-in content creator for blog posts and landing pages. The Startup Package ($1,499) gives you a professional website with growth-ready architecture. The Basic Package ($499) is for clients who only need a simple, straightforward website.
How does the pay-as-you-grow model work?
For the Startup and Enterprise packages, you pay 50% upfront for your website. The remaining balance can be paid through a flexible monthly plan. After launch, you can request additional features like automation, AI chatbots, or CRM systems. Each capability is developed and paid for separately.
What is included in the Enterprise SEO setup?
The Enterprise Package includes a full SEO audit, keyword research for your industry, meta tag optimization, Google Analytics setup, and a content strategy. You also get a content creator for blog posts, landing pages, product descriptions, and more.
Do I need to buy everything from day one?
No. The core principle is that you start with what you need today. Your website is built on a growth-ready foundation, so when you are ready to add automation, AI, or other capabilities, they integrate seamlessly without rebuilding.
How long does a typical project take?
Most websites go live within 2 to 3 weeks. The Basic Package is typically faster due to its simpler scope. Need it faster? Ask about our priority track.
Can I upgrade from Basic to Startup or Enterprise later?
Yes. If you start with the Basic Package and later decide you want growth capabilities, we can migrate you to the Startup or Enterprise Package foundation. Your existing website is preserved and enhanced, not rebuilt.
Do I need any technical skills?
None at all. We handle everything from setup to launch and give you a simple dashboard to manage things afterward.
Do you offer ongoing support?
Yes. Every project includes dedicated post-launch support, with optional maintenance plans for long-term peace of mind.
