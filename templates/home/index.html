Content

Search that understands. Content that gets discovered.

OnWebApp combines intelligent search with AI-ready content systems to help websites deliver better answers to users and improve how content is discovered across traditional search engines and generative AI platforms.

AI-Powered Search

  • Instant Answers — transform search into an experience that responds like a knowledgeable support agent
  • Conversational Search — let users search naturally using complete questions and everyday language
  • Context-Aware Results — go beyond keyword matching using page content, metadata, and user intent
  • Intelligent Product Discovery — help users find products using natural descriptions and attributes

Content Built for Search and AI

  • GEO-Optimized Content — improve visibility across generative search and AI answer engines
  • AI-Ready Content Architecture — organize content into clear entities, topics, and structured information
  • Automated Content Generation — generate pages, FAQs, and descriptions at scale with consistent brand guidelines
  • Structured Data & Schema — help search engines understand pages, products, and organizations
From Search to Answer

Understand → Retrieve → Generate → Optimize — connects intelligent search and content generation into one workflow, helping businesses create digital experiences that are easier for people — and AI systems — to understand.

Integration

Connect every system. Orchestrate every operation.

Bring your business applications, APIs, data sources, IoT devices, and external platforms together through a connected integration layer. OnWebApp helps businesses move data between systems, automate processes, and maintain a consistent source of truth across their operations.

Plug & Play Integration

  • 50+ Connectors — connect Shopify, Stripe, Salesforce, and other services without rebuilding infrastructure
  • API-First Architecture — build custom integrations with REST APIs, webhooks, and secure data exchange
  • Flexible Integration — connect cloud apps, internal systems, databases, and business tools

Unified Business Operations

  • ERP & CRM Synchronization — keep customer, product, inventory, and financial data synchronized
  • Data Integration — move data between applications while reducing duplicates and manual entry
  • IoT & Device Integration — connect sensors, kiosks, and equipment to business applications
  • B2B Integration — connect suppliers, partners, and marketplaces through structured APIs
Integration That Works With Your Stack

Connect → Synchronize → Automate → Monitor — whether you are connecting an ERP, synchronizing e-commerce data, integrating an API, or bringing IoT devices into your workflow, OnWebApp provides the integration layer that keeps systems connected.

Automation

Turn processes into systems that run themselves.

Automate repetitive work, coordinate complex business processes, and connect actions across websites, applications, enterprise systems, and connected devices. OnWebApp turns business rules into workflows that execute consistently with less manual intervention.

Website & Business Automation

  • Workflow Automation — create workflows that respond to events, conditions, and business rules automatically
  • Customer Journeys — build automated journeys connecting interactions, data, and actions across the lifecycle
  • Scheduled Operations — run recurring tasks automatically based on schedules or predefined conditions
  • Intelligent Actions — combine rules, APIs, data, and AI-powered decisions that adapt to business situations

Enterprise Automation

  • Cross-System Workflows — orchestrate processes across websites, CRM, ERP, APIs, and databases
  • RPA & Browser Automation — automate repetitive browser and computer-based tasks
  • Robots & Kiosks — bridge digital workflows with physical environments through connected devices
  • Industrial Automation — connect operational data and automation across industrial environments
From Trigger to Action

Detect → Decide → Execute → Monitor — a workflow can start from a user action, API request, scheduled event, system change, or sensor signal — then execute actions across connected systems.

AI

Intelligence that understands, predicts, and acts.

AI is built into OnWebApp to help businesses understand data, anticipate patterns, generate useful content, and turn insights into automated actions. From customer behavior to operational workflows, AI works alongside your existing systems rather than sitting apart from them.

Predictive Intelligence

  • Behavior Prediction — analyze behavioral patterns to identify potential customer needs and opportunities
  • Intelligent Segmentation — group customers, products, or data based on behavior and predicted intent
  • Anomaly Detection — identify unusual activity, unexpected changes, and patterns requiring attention
  • Predictive Insights — turn historical and real-time data into insights that identify trends earlier

Generative AI

  • AI-Powered Workflows — use AI to interpret information, make decisions, and support business processes
  • Content Generation — generate pages, descriptions, summaries, FAQs, and reports at scale
  • AI Recommendations — transform data into actionable recommendations for content and operations
  • AI-Generated Reports — turn large amounts of data into structured summaries and insights
From Intelligence to Action

Understand → Predict → Generate → Automate — OnWebApp connects AI with your websites, business systems, data, and workflows so intelligence can move beyond analysis and become part of everyday operations.
